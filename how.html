Our PRINCIPLES

How we do it

CATALYST has been developed by educators to provide you and your colleagues with a simple way to audit any/all areas of provision in your school. The software offers a simple input interface and instantly provides feedback in a shareable format. This clean approach makes CATALYST easy to embed in every school.

Using Catalyst

Get up and running in moments... simply assign an audit to a subject leader or department head and wait for the results to appear.

We're here to help

To make it even easier for you to get started, we offer every new Catalyst member a free set-up service. Simply send us the list of staff and the audits you would like them to take, and we'll do the rest.

Sector benchmarking
