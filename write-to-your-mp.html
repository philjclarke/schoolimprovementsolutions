Fund Schools Fairly

Write to your MP

Please help us to raise this issue with your Local MP. This is a key method for raising the interest of politicians and help us to get a debate in Parliament on this vital issue.

Ask them to sign the petition and get back in touch with their plans to help to Fund Schools Fairly.

You can find you're MP's email address at https://members.parliament.uk/FindYourMP

This is what we'll send to your MP.

Dear [MP’s name],
I am writing as a school leader in your constituency to ask you to support — and add your name to — a petition calling for a Parliamentary debate to ensure we as a country Fund Schools Fairly.
Schools are currently funded per pupil, yet our real costs do not fall neatly when pupil numbers change. Staffing, SEND provision, safeguarding and leadership capacity are largely fixed — and since Covid, schools have absorbed growing responsibilities once held by other services.
Independent analysis shows that per-pupil funding is set to fall further in real terms over the coming years, while pay rises are not fully funded. This is not inefficiency; it is structural underfunding.
Well-funded schools are one of the strongest protections we have for the future — economically, socially and nationally.
I urge you to support this debate and to sign the petition calling for a funding model that reflects real operating need.
Kind regards,
