The funding gap is already baked in

Independent analysis from the Institute for Fiscal Studies (IFS) shows that, under current plans, school funding per pupil is forecast to be around 4.9% lower in real terms by 2028–29 once rising costs and SEND pressures are accounted for. This means schools are being asked to do more, for longer, with less purchasing power each year.

Read the IFS analysis

Parents are being asked to plug the gaps

A national poll of 6,199 parents, reported by Schools Week, found that two in five (41%) have been asked to contribute to schools’ day-to-day running costs — not extras, but basics. This is a clear sign that school budgets are no longer covering real operating needs.

Read the Schools Week report

Pay rises without funding force cuts elsewhere

The Treasury has confirmed that teacher pay rises will not be fully funded, meaning schools must absorb the cost from existing budgets. In practice, this forces schools to cut support staff, reduce services, or stretch teachers further just to stand still.

Report by TES

What is Catalyst?

Catalyst is a tool that provides schools with the tools to track, monitor and focus their school improvement journey. With features such as goal setting, progress tracking, and data analysis, Catalyst helps schools to identify areas for improvement and monitor progress over time.

Catalyst also offers a collaborative platform where teachers, administrators, and other stakeholders can work together to achieve common goals creating agency for staff across your organisation. With regular updates and custom reports, Catalyst helps schools to stay on track and reach their full potential.
